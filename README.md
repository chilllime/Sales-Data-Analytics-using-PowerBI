# Sales-Data-Analytics-using-PowerBI
Sales Dashboard 
## Overview

This project involves analyzing the sales data of Adventure Works using Power BI to generate insights that can drive business decisions. The analysis focuses on various Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as monthly returns, monthly and yearly revenue, profits, top-selling products, product subcategories, and customer behavior.

## Objectives

To visualize and monitor sales performance across different dimensions.

To identify trends and patterns in customer behavior.

To highlight top-performing products and categories.

To enable data-driven decision-making through interactive dashboards.

## Features

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

Monthly Returns: Analysis of product returns on a monthly basis to identify patterns and improve return management.

Monthly and Yearly Revenue: Visualization of revenue trends to monitor growth and detect seasonal fluctuations.

Profit Analysis: Calculation of gross and net profits to assess the financial health of the business.

Product Performance:

Identification of top-selling products and their respective subcategories.

Analysis of underperforming products for potential improvement.

Customer Behavior:

Insights into purchasing habits, including frequency and average order value.

Segmentation of customers based on demographics and purchase patterns.

Interactive Dashboards:

User-friendly visualizations that allow filtering and drilling down into specific data points.

Overview pages summarizing key insights.

## Data Sources

The dataset used for this analysis is derived from Adventure Works’ transactional data, which includes:

Sales transactions

Customer details

Product information

Returns data

## Tools and Technologies

Power BI: For data modeling, visualization, and dashboard creation.

Excel/CSV: Data preprocessing and validation.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): For creating calculated columns and measures.
